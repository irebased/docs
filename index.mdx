## Welcome to CipherInspector Docs!

CipherInspector is a cryptanalysis dashboarding tool that enables you to create a
single view for all your analysis. There are many tools offered, all of which are
documented here to help you understand how they work. There is currently a plan to
also open source all of the formulas to get rid of any guess work.

CipherInspector comes in two tiers: Basic and Enigma. The Basic tier is completely
free and allows you to create dashboards in the CipherInspector playground. These
dashboards are not saved to your account and cannot be shared via share link.
You can also view public dashboards in the Basic plan and copy them to your playground
to mess with them.

The Enigma plan is a monthly subscription, primarily offering convenience features
that will enable you to:
- Create and save dashboards to your account
- Create and share public dashboards with others
- Copy playground and public dashboards to your profile

If you cancel your Enigma subscription, it will last until the expiration date of the
subscription. Once expired, dashboards on your account will become read-only, with the
ability to delete the dashboards if you desire.

## How to use CipherInspector

All you need to do is sign into CipherInspector using Google or Discord OAuth, and you can start
building your dashboards.
