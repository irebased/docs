## Foundational

These widgets perform basic tests that are considered the foundation of many
advanced formulas.

- [Frequency Analysis](/widgets/frequency-analysis)
- [Index of Coincidence](/widgets/index-of-coincidence)
- [Kasiski Examination](/widgets/kasiski-test)
- [Missing Characters](/widgets/missing-characters)

## Bitstream

These widgets are particularly interested in the bits/bytes which are used
to encode your ciphertext.

- [ASCII Distribution Chart](/widgets/ascii-distribution-chart)
- [Bitstream Variance](/widgets/bitstream-variance)

## Randomness tests

These widgets are used to test how random your ciphertext is.

- [Shannon Entropy](/widgets/shannon-entropy)
- [Expected Bin Occupancy](/widgets/expected-bin-occupancy)

## Comparison

These widgets are used to compare your ciphertext against a base dataset. This
is typically either English or another ciphertext from your dashboard.

- [Normal Order](/widgets/normor)
- [Chi-Squared Test](/widgets/chi-squared-test)
- [Kolmogorov-Smirnov Test](/widgets/ks-test)
- [Levenshtein Distance](/widgets/levenshtein-distance)

## Display

These widgets are used to help format the dashboard and segment information.

- [Title](/widgets/title)
